# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful. Disagree on technical points, not people. No harassment, personal
attacks, or discriminatory language, in issues, PRs, or anywhere else in this project's
spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/pzverkov/shiftly-rn/security/advisories/new)
or contacting the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block contributors who violate
this standard, at their discretion.
